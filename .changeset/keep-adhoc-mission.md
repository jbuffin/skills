At the end of a run on an ad hoc mission, Commodore now offers to keep it. Outside Admiral it asks once whether to keep it user-global, keep it for the repo, or drop it, and `mission.py keep` saves the validated file into that layer without overwriting anything. Inside Admiral the Admiral report gains an optional `missions` field carrying the proposal for Admiral's Inbox. A saved mission then shows up in `mission.py list` for later runs.
