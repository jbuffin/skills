New `retro` skill: a retrospective on one Admiral panel's autopilot Commodore runs. `retro-analyze.py` reads the saved `admiral outcomes` records and the runs' `state.md` files and writes `analysis.json` and `retro.md`: stops and engineer edits that repeat across two or more runs, each with evidence, a cause and one proposed change (an allow rule, a skill edit, a panel setting, or none). The skill opens one draft PR only for a skill edit, and under autopilot it asks through the inbox and ends its Admiral report with a `retro` record that the next retro starts from.
